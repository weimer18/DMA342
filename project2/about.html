About Me

Hi! I'm Jake Weimer, a DMA major at Canisius University in Buffalo, NY.

I began my journey in editing when I was very young. I always loved seeing the different editing styles that many of the YouTubers I followed used, and it inspired me to start learning how to edit on my own, so that I could one day bejust like those I watched online. I am currently in Canisius as a DMA Major studying different softwares and techniques so that I can have all the experience I need to be able to create my own entertaining edits!
